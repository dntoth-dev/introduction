About me

Hey,

I am a 17 year old student from Budapest, Hungary. I joined HackClub in 2025 with Summer of Making, and this is the second programme where I signed up. I've been involved in programming and software development since the age 11. I started off with block-programming, learned a tiny bit of JavaScript, I experimented with C#, but I must mention my Python knowledge. I do everything in Python, I have learned it for 4 years, and it truly is my favourite programming language. Although I really enjoy developing in Python, I always realise, that there are many areas where Python is not always efficiently applicable, so I'm interested in learning other languages as well.

In the future, I'd like to work in the field of Aerospace Engineering, as aviation and airplanes are interesting topics for me since a very young age, and since then, I learned many things about them, including Physics, operational procedures, their propulsion systems, and the infrastructure that holds them all together, and I enjoy reading in this topic too.
